Shipping and Delivery

How orders leave our factory in Yiwu, who arranges what, and where risk passes.

Incoterms

Quotations are issued on FOB Yiwu terms unless the proforma invoice states a different Incoterm. Under FOB the buyer arranges and pays for main carriage, insurance and import clearance.

Where you ask us to quote CIF, CFR, EXW or DDP, that will be stated on the proforma invoice and the price will reflect it. We do not arrange import clearance in your country on your behalf.

Production lead time

Lead time is quoted per order, because it depends on the frame, the colour, the lens option and the packaging rather than on the style alone.

Stock colours move faster than a new Pantone match, and a printed box is usually the slowest single item in the chain. Where a delivery date matters, tell us at quotation stage and we will build the schedule around it in writing.

Consolidation

Several references can be consolidated into one shipment, which is usually the cheapest way to move a mixed order. Consolidation must be agreed before the goods are packed, because carton markings and the packing list are prepared for the shipment as a whole.

Documents

A standard export set is issued with each shipment: commercial invoice, packing list, and the bill of lading or airway bill. Certificates of origin or other documents required by your customs authority are available on request and must be asked for before shipment, not after.

Risk and inspection

Risk passes in accordance with the Incoterm on the invoice. Under FOB, risk passes when the goods are loaded on board at the port of shipment.

We inspect goods before packing. If you want a third-party inspection, tell us before the production completion date so the inspection can be booked without delaying the shipment. Inspection fees are borne by the buyer.

Duties, taxes and destination charges

Import duty, value added tax, consumption tax and any destination handling charge are the buyer's responsibility and are not included in our prices.

Where a shipment is delayed or returned because the buyer has not provided the information needed for import, the resulting storage, return freight and re-export costs are the buyer's responsibility.

Delays outside our control

We are not liable for delay caused by events outside our reasonable control, including carrier disruption, port congestion, customs examination, epidemic control measures or a shortage of a raw material that could not reasonably be foreseen. We will tell you as soon as we know of a delay and agree a revised date.
