Returns and Refunds

What to do when goods arrive damaged, short or not as approved, and which items cannot be returned.

Claim window

Please inspect the goods on arrival. Any claim for shortage, damage, wrong item or deviation from the approved sample must reach us within 7 days of receipt, with photographs of the carton label and the affected pieces.

Claims received after that window may still be considered, but we cannot accept a claim once the goods have been sold on, altered or used in production.

What we will do

Where the goods do not match the approved sample or the proforma invoice, we will, at your choice and after confirming the defect, repair the affected pieces, replace them in the next production run, or credit their invoiced value.

Where the issue affects a large part of a shipment we will discuss a return of the affected cartons. Return freight on a confirmed defect is arranged with you in advance; please do not ship goods back before we have agreed the method.

What is not covered

Customised goods — frames with your logo, your Pantone colour, your mould or your printed packaging — cannot be returned for a change of mind, because they cannot be resold.

Normal variation in a moulded or acetate frame, such as a slight difference in tone between production lots or a minor polish mark inside the temple, is not a defect.

Damage caused after delivery, by the carrier if the shipment was insured by the buyer, by improper storage, or by resale in a market the product was not specified for, is not covered by us.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds are processed through Stripe and generally appear within five to ten business days.

Where an order has been partly delivered, the refund is limited to the value of the affected pieces and their share of freight, not to the whole invoice.

Warranty against manufacturing defect

Beyond the 7-day claim window, we stand behind the frame itself against manufacturing defects — a hinge that fails under normal use, a lens that delaminates, a temple that cracks at the joint — reported within 12 months of shipment.

This does not cover wear, scratching, accidental damage, or damage from cleaning with solvents. Claims under this paragraph are resolved by replacement in a following order, not by cash refund.

How to raise a claim

Write to josie@boyarn.com with the invoice number, the reference, the quantity affected and photographs. We aim to reply within two business days with a decision and a remedy.
