Cookies and Local Storage

The short version: no advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking. Below is the detail of what is stored and why.

What we set ourselves

We do not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

When you press the like button on a product page we store a single flag in your browser's local storage, named like boyarn-like-BY-009. Its only purpose is to stop the counter increasing twice from the same browser. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

What our providers set

Cloudflare. Our host and CDN sets security and performance cookies such as __cf_bm and cf_clearance. These are used to tell humans from automated traffic and to keep the site available; they are not used for advertising.

Stripe. If you choose card payment you are taken to Stripe's checkout. Stripe sets its own cookies there to process the payment and to detect fraud. Those cookies are governed by Stripe's cookie and privacy policies, not ours.

Google Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google's font service, which receives your IP address as part of serving the file.

What we do not do

No advertising or remarketing pixels, and no third-party analytics profile that follows you across other sites.

No selling of behavioural data. Our review and like data is described in the Privacy Policy and is limited to what is needed to publish a moderated review and to prevent abuse.

Controlling storage

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking the Cloudflare security cookie may cause the site to challenge or refuse your requests. Blocking storage means the like button may count the same visitor more than once, which affects nobody but us.

Questions

Write to josie@boyarn.com if you want to know what a particular cookie on a particular page is doing.
