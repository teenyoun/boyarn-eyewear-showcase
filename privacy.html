Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use boyarn.com, write to us, or leave a review, and what we do with it.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Stellar Specs Limited, Room S051, 2/F, Capital Plaza, 61-65 Chatham Road South, Tsim Sha Tsui, Hong Kong. Questions about this policy, or any request about your data, can be sent to josie@boyarn.com.

What we collect

Enquiry data. When you contact us by email, WhatsApp or a form we receive your name, contact details, company, country and whatever you choose to tell us about your requirement.

Review data. If you submit a buyer review we store the display name, country, rating, review text and optional email address you provide, together with a truncated cryptographic hash of your IP address used only to limit abuse. Your email address is never published.

Like counts. Our like button stores a count per style. To stop one visitor inflating the count we store a hash derived from your IP address and browser string. The hash is one-way; we cannot recover your IP address from it, and it is not linked to any identity.

Technical data. Our hosting and security providers log the requests made to this site, including IP address, browser and timestamp, for security and performance purposes.

We do not knowingly collect data from children, and we do not process payment card numbers on this site (see Payment Terms).

Why we use it

To answer enquiries, prepare quotations and fulfil orders.

To publish moderated buyer reviews, which is done on the basis of your consent at the point of submission and withdrawn if you ask us to remove a review.

To keep the site available and secure, and to prevent spam and abuse.

To meet accounting, tax and record-keeping obligations that apply to us in Hong Kong.

Who else sees it

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

Our service providers process data on our instructions: Cloudflare provides hosting, content delivery, security and the database behind reviews and likes; Google provides search indexing only through publicly accessible pages; our email provider carries correspondence. Where you choose to pay by card, Stripe processes the payment and receives the billing details you enter on its checkout page — we never see your card number.

We may disclose data where we are legally required to, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

Where it is stored and for how long

Data may be stored or processed outside Hong Kong, including in the European Union, the United States and the Asia-Pacific region, on infrastructure run by the providers named above.

Enquiry and order records are kept for seven years to satisfy Hong Kong accounting requirements. Review submissions that are not approved are deleted within 90 days. Rejected reviews are kept only long enough to prevent re-submission of the same content.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or ask us to stop using it for a particular purpose. You may withdraw consent to publication of a review at any time, and we will remove it from the site.

Write to josie@boyarn.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to the review database is restricted to the operator, and reviews are published only after manual approval. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to the minimum needed to run the business.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the foot of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
