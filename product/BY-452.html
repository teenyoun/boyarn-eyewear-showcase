Build notes

How this frame is made and bought

The frame is produced on our own tooling in Yiwu, which is what keeps a one-piece sample and a 2,000-piece run on the same geometry. The finish is the part that changes with volume: hand polishing is the first step that gets shortened, so specify it. It ships Individual box.

The lens is a plain optical lens with no special coating catalogued. That is not a weakness: for a private-label line it means the lens can be swapped for your own specification without changing the frame, and the price you see is a frame price rather than a lens price.

There is no fixed colour list on this one. The frame is finished to your reference, which means the first decision on an order is the colour rather than the quantity.

Minimum order is 1 pc per style. Pricing is quoted FOB Yiwu across 1 quantity tiers, so the price you see at the top of the page is the top of the ladder rather than a single figure. Logo and packaging are open to your artwork. Standard packing is Individual box. Samples are available before production; quote the reference and we reply with price, MOQ and lead time.

Carton quantity is worth checking against your own warehouse: a large carton lowers freight per unit and raises the chance of damage at the bottom layer, and the inner packing is what decides which of those two wins. If a style is going into a retail programme, ask for a bulk photo before shipment rather than after arrival. Colour drift between the sample and the bulk lot is the most common dispute on a first order, and it is cheap to catch early.
