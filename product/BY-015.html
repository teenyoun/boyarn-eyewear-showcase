Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Front and fit

    A single-piece frame cut for men and women, sized so the lens sits inside the cheekbone line rather than resting on it.

  • 02

    Stainless steel frame

    Made in stainless steel, which gives corrosion resistance and a slim, springy temple. At 60 g it stays light enough to wear all day.

  • 03

    UV400 lens

    The lens is UV400 in PC, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    At 60 g it is light enough to wear through a working day without the temple arms digging in.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by heat transfer or screen printing, with position and size agreed at sampling. Ships 1 pc / bag, 12 pcs / box; carton footprint 16.5 x 5.5 x 5 cm. Minimum order 2 pieces.
