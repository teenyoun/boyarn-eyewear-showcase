Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Square full rim

    A straight brow line and a squared lower rim. The square front suits square, oval and heart-shaped faces best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

  • 02

    Metal frame

    Made in metal, which gives a thin profile and adjustable nose arms, so the fit can be set precisely.

  • 03

    UV400 lens

    The lens is UV400 in PC, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    Temple tips and nose arms are sized to hold without pinching, which is what decides whether a frame gets worn or left in a drawer.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by a metal plaque or laser engraving, with position and size agreed at sampling. Ships 1 pc / bag, 12 pcs / carton. Minimum order 1 pieces.
