Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Square full rim

    A straight brow line and a squared lower rim. The square front suits square, oval and heart-shaped faces best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

  • 02

    Polycarbonate frame

    Made in polycarbonate, which gives a light, impact-resistant frame that holds up at an entry price point.

  • 03

    Polarised / UV400 lens

    The lens is Polarised / UV400 in TAC, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    Temple tips and nose arms are sized to hold without pinching, which is what decides whether a frame gets worn or left in a drawer.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by hot foil stamping or heat transfer, with position and size agreed at sampling. Ships 1 pc / bag, 12 pcs / carton. Minimum order 1 pieces.

Build notes

How this frame is made and bought

The frame is a square full-rim design for adults. It sits in the general line, which in practice means it has to carry a retail range across a season rather than chase one trend.

The catalogued colours are Black, Gold, White and Grey. On a private-label run these are a starting point rather than a limit: colour is matched to a sample or a Pantone reference, and a matched colour adds roughly two weeks to the lead time because the masterbatch has to be run before moulding or sheet cutting begins.

Check the lens groove before the finish: if the groove is tight the lens sits under stress and crazes at the edge within a season, which is a return rather than a complaint.

A polycarbonate front keeps the tooling cost down and the weight low, and it takes colour through the material instead of on top of it, so a scuff does not turn into a white line. Check the hinge area and the lens groove on arrival: those are where a short moulding cycle shows first. The lenses are TAC. It ships 1 pc / bag, 12 pcs / carton.

Minimum order is 1 pc per style. Pricing is quoted FOB Yiwu across 3 quantity tiers, so the price you see at the top of the page is the top of the ladder rather than a single figure.
