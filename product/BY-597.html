Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Square full rim

    A straight brow line and a squared lower rim. The square front suits square, oval and heart-shaped faces best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

  • 02

    Polycarbonate frame

    Made in polycarbonate, which gives a light, impact-resistant frame that holds up at an entry price point. At 44 g it stays light enough to wear all day.

  • 03

    UV400 lens

    The lens is UV400 in PC, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    At 44 g it is light enough to wear through a working day without the temple arms digging in.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by laser engraving or hot foil stamping, with position and size agreed at sampling. Ships 1 pc / bag, 12 pcs / carton. Minimum order 1 pieces.
