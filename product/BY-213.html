Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Square full rim

    A straight brow line and a squared lower rim. The square front suits square, oval and heart-shaped faces best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

  • 02

    Frame build

    Temples, hinges and nose arms are aligned by hand in our Yiwu workshop, so the frame closes evenly and the hinge tension is set before packing.

  • 03

    Lens and fitting

    Lenses are cut and fitted in-house, so rim tension is set correctly instead of being forced at assembly.

  • 04

    How it wears

    Built to survive daily handling.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by a metal plaque or laser engraving, with position and size agreed at sampling. Packed to a carton footprint of 20 x 10 x 5 cm.
