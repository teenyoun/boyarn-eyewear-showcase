Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Round full rim

    A round eye wire and a shallow lens depth. The round front suits a strong jaw and angular features best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

  • 02

    Frame build

    A full rim construction. At 60 g it stays light enough to wear all day. Size and fit are confirmed with the quotation for this style.

  • 03

    UV400 lens

    The lens is UV400, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    Shaped for long wear, light enough to forget you have it on and built to survive daily handling.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by screen printing or a metal plaque, with position and size agreed at sampling. Ships 1 pc / OPP bag; carton footprint 16.5 x 5.5 x 5 cm.
