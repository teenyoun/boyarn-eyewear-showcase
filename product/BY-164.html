Build notes

How this frame is made and bought

This is a plain frame. It sits in the general line, which in practice means it has to carry a retail range across a season rather than chase one trend.

The lens is a plain optical lens with no special coating catalogued. That is not a weakness: for a private-label line it means the lens can be swapped for your own specification without changing the frame, and the price you see is a frame price rather than a lens price.

There is no fixed colour list on this one. The frame is finished to your reference, which means the first decision on an order is the colour rather than the quantity.

The inspection points on a moulded frame are the lens groove, the hinge screws and the temple ends. A short moulding cycle shows as a rough inner rim before it shows anywhere else, so that is the first thing to check on a delivered carton.

Minimum order is 1 pc per style. Pricing is quoted FOB Yiwu across 2 quantity tiers, so the price you see at the top of the page is the top of the ladder rather than a single figure. Logo and packaging are open to your artwork. Standard packing is Individual box. Samples are available before production; quote the reference and we reply with price, MOQ and lead time.

Nose pads are the other wear item. On a metal frame they are replaceable, which is a service argument for an optician channel; on a moulded frame the pad is part of the front, so the fit has to be right at the sample stage. Temple logos are laser-etched on metal and hot-stamped on moulded frames; neither adds meaningful cost at volume, but both need artwork approved before the run. A printed box is a longer lead item than the frame itself.
