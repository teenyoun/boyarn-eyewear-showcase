Build notes

How this frame is made and bought

On a moulded front the variables are cycle time and colour masterbatch. A fast cycle leaves weld lines across the brow and a sink mark behind the bridge; both are visible in raking light and neither shows in a studio photograph.

Production runs on our own tooling, so the sample you approve and the bulk delivery come off the same geometry. Ask for a bulk photo before shipment; colour drift between lots is the usual complaint on a repeat order. It ships 1 pc / OPP bag.

UV400 is the specification that matters on a sunglasses order: it means the lens blocks wavelengths up to 400 nm, which covers the whole UVA and UVB band rather than only the burn-causing part of it. A lens can be dark without being UV400, so the two are not the same claim and should not be sold as the same claim.

Sampling starts at one piece, with the volume ladder in the price block above setting the break points. Pricing is quoted FOB Yiwu across 3 quantity tiers, so the price you see at the top of the page is the top of the ladder rather than a single figure. Standard packing is 1 pc / OPP bag. Samples are available before production; quote the reference and we reply with price, MOQ and lead time.

Volume pricing is set per style, so a mixed order of several references reaches a lower unit cost slower than a single reference. Buyers who need a range usually accept one tier lower and take the breadth instead. Lead time on a repeat order is driven by colour and packaging, not by the frame: the tooling is already set, so a same-colour rerun moves faster than a new colourway and a printed box is the slowest single item in the chain.
