Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Front and fit

    A single-piece frame cut to a women's fit, sized so the lens sits inside the cheekbone line rather than resting on it.

  • 02

    Polycarbonate frame

    Made in polycarbonate, which gives a light, impact-resistant frame that holds up at an entry price point.
