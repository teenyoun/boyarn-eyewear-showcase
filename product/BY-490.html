Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Square full rim

    A straight brow line and a squared lower rim. The square front suits square, oval and heart-shaped faces best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

  • 02

    Frame build

    A full rim construction. At 45 g it stays light enough to wear all day. Size and fit are confirmed with the quotation for this style.

  • 03

    UV400 lens

    The lens is UV400, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    At 45 g it is light enough to wear through a working day without the temple arms digging in.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by heat transfer or screen printing, with position and size agreed at sampling. Ships 1 pc / OPP bag; carton footprint 16 x 5 x 4 cm.
