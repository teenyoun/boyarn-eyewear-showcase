Build notes

How this frame is made and bought

The frame is a plain design for unisex adults. It sits in the general line, which in practice means it has to carry a retail range across a season rather than chase one trend.

Black ship as standard. Because the frame is produced on our own tooling, a colour change is a production decision rather than a new style: it goes through a colour match first, which is why it is the packaging and the colour that set the lead time, not the frame.

Check the lens groove before the finish: if the groove is tight the lens sits under stress and crazes at the edge within a season, which is a return rather than a complaint.

PC moulds to a consistent wall thickness, which is why the same tool can run a matte and a glossy finish without changing the geometry. On a volume order the variable to lock down is colour masterbatch, not shape. The lenses are AC.

Sampling starts at one piece, with the volume ladder in the price block above setting the break points. Pricing is quoted FOB Yiwu across 2 quantity tiers, so the price you see at the top of the page is the top of the ladder rather than a single figure. Logo and packaging are open to your artwork. Samples are available before production; quote the reference and we reply with price, MOQ and lead time.

Carton quantity is worth checking against your own warehouse: a large carton lowers freight per unit and raises the chance of damage at the bottom layer, and the inner packing is what decides which of those two wins. If a style is going into a retail programme, ask for a bulk photo before shipment rather than after arrival. Colour drift between the sample and the bulk lot is the most common dispute on a first order, and it is cheap to catch early.
