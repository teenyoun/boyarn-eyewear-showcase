Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Square full rim

    A straight brow line and a squared lower rim. The square front suits square, oval and heart-shaped faces best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

    A full rim construction. Size and fit are confirmed with the quotation for this style.

Build notes

How this frame is made and bought

There is no fixed colour list on this one. The frame is finished to your reference, which means the first decision on an order is the colour rather than the quantity.

The inspection points on a moulded frame are the lens groove, the hinge screws and the temple ends. A short moulding cycle shows as a rough inner rim before it shows anywhere else, so that is the first thing to check on a delivered carton.

The frame is produced on our own tooling in Yiwu, which is what keeps a one-piece sample and a 2,000-piece run on the same geometry. The finish is the part that changes with volume: hand polishing is the first step that gets shortened, so specify it. The pattern is solid, carried through the front.

Sampling starts at one piece, with the volume ladder in the price block above setting the break points. Pricing is quoted FOB Yiwu across 3 quantity tiers, so the price you see at the top of the page is the top of the ladder rather than a single figure. Samples are available before production; quote the reference and we reply with price, MOQ and lead time.

Volume pricing is set per style, so a mixed order of several references reaches a lower unit cost slower than a single reference. Buyers who need a range usually accept one tier lower and take the breadth instead. Lead time on a repeat order is driven by colour and packaging, not by the frame: the tooling is already set, so a same-colour rerun moves faster than a new colourway and a printed box is the slowest single item in the chain.
