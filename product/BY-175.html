Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Square full rim

    A straight brow line and a squared lower rim. The square front suits square, oval and heart-shaped faces best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

  • 02

    Frame build

    A full rim construction. At 60 g it stays light enough to wear all day. Size and fit are confirmed with the quotation for this style.

  • 03

    PC lens

    Supplied with a PC lens.

  • 04

    How it wears

    Cut to shade the skin around the eye as well as the eye itself.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by a metal plaque or laser engraving, with position and size agreed at sampling. Packed to a carton footprint of 19 x 7 x 7 cm.
