Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Rectangle full rim

    A low brow line and a narrow rectangle profile. The rectangle front suits wide faces and longer nose bridges best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

  • 02

    Frame build

    A full rim construction. At 50 g it stays light enough to wear all day. Size and fit are confirmed with the quotation for this style.

  • 03

    UV400 lens

    The lens is UV400, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    Built to survive daily handling, light enough to forget you have it on and shaped for long wear.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by laser engraving or hot foil stamping, with position and size agreed at sampling. Ships 1 pc / OPP bag; carton footprint 16.5 x 5.5 x 5 cm.
