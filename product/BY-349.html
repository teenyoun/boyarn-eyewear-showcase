Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Front and fit

    A single-piece frame cut for men and women, sized so the lens sits inside the cheekbone line rather than resting on it.

  • 02

    Polycarbonate frame

    Made in polycarbonate, which gives a light, impact-resistant frame that holds up at an entry price point. At 60 g it stays light enough to wear all day.

  • 03

    UV400 lens

    The lens is UV400 in AC, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    At 60 g it is light enough to wear through a working day without the temple arms digging in.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by a metal plaque or laser engraving, with position and size agreed at sampling. Packed to a carton footprint of 17 x 6 x 5 cm.
