Build notes

How this frame is made and bought

On a moulded front the variables are cycle time and colour masterbatch. A fast cycle leaves weld lines across the brow and a sink mark behind the bridge; both are visible in raking light and neither shows in a studio photograph.

Production runs on our own tooling, so the sample you approve and the bulk delivery come off the same geometry. Ask for a bulk photo before shipment; colour drift between lots is the usual complaint on a repeat order. The pattern is solid, carried through the front.

The lens is a plain optical lens with no special coating catalogued. That is not a weakness: for a private-label line it means the lens can be swapped for your own specification without changing the frame, and the price you see is a frame price rather than a lens price.

Sampling starts at one piece, with the volume ladder in the price block above setting the break points. Pricing is quoted FOB Yiwu across 3 quantity tiers, so the price you see at the top of the page is the top of the ladder rather than a single figure. Samples are available before production; quote the reference and we reply with price, MOQ and lead time.

The cheapest way to test a style is a small paid sample against your own lens and packaging, not a bulk order with a colour change. Colour and print are the two decisions that cannot be reversed after the run starts. Carton quantity is worth checking against your own warehouse: a large carton lowers freight per unit and raises the chance of damage at the bottom layer, and the inner packing is what decides which of those two wins.
