Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Square full rim

    A straight brow line and a squared lower rim. The square front suits square, oval and heart-shaped faces best, and keeps the lens width inside the cheekbone line.

Build notes

How this frame is made and bought

The lens is a plain optical lens with no special coating catalogued. That is not a weakness: for a private-label line it means the lens can be swapped for your own specification without changing the frame, and the price you see is a frame price rather than a lens price.

There is no fixed colour list on this one. The frame is finished to your reference, which means the first decision on an order is the colour rather than the quantity.

Check the lens groove before the finish: if the groove is tight the lens sits under stress and crazes at the edge within a season, which is a return rather than a complaint.

Sampling starts at one piece, with the volume ladder in the price block above setting the break points. Pricing is quoted FOB Yiwu across 3 quantity tiers, so the price you see at the top of the page is the top of the ladder rather than a single figure. Samples are available before production; quote the reference and we reply with price, MOQ and lead time.

If a style is going into a retail programme, ask for a bulk photo before shipment rather than after arrival. Colour drift between the sample and the bulk lot is the most common dispute on a first order, and it is cheap to catch early. Nose pads are the other wear item. On a metal frame they are replaceable, which is a service argument for an optician channel; on a moulded frame the pad is part of the front, so the fit has to be right at the sample stage.
