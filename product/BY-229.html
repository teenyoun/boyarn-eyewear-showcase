Style notes

Five things worth knowing

  • 01

    Front and fit

    A children's frame cut for a child's face, sized so the lens sits inside the cheekbone line rather than resting on it — important on a growing face.

  • 02

    Metal frame

    Made in metal, which gives a thin profile and adjustable nose arms, so the fit can be set precisely. At 45 g it stays light enough to wear all day.

  • 03

    UV400 lens

    The lens is UV400 in PC, and blocks UVA and UVB across the full spectrum.

  • 04

    How it wears

    At 45 g it is light enough to wear through a working day without the temple arms digging in.

  • 05

    Your mark on it

    Logo can be applied by laser engraving or hot foil stamping, with position and size agreed at sampling. Packed to a carton footprint of 16 x 5 x 4 cm.
