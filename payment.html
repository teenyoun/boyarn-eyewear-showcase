Payment Terms

How payment works for a wholesale or OEM order, and what to expect once card payment goes live on this site.

Payment is made against a proforma invoice

Every order starts from a proforma invoice. It states the references, quantities, unit prices, Incoterm, total amount, currency and the payment schedule that applies to your order.

Production is scheduled once the deposit shown on the invoice has cleared. Where an order is small enough to be paid in full up front, the invoice will say so instead of showing a split.

Card payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe on a hosted checkout page. When you choose card payment you leave our site and complete the transaction on Stripe's secure page.

We never receive or store your full card number, expiry date or security code. We receive only the confirmation that payment succeeded, the amount, the currency and the billing name and country needed for our accounts.

Stripe's own privacy policy governs the data you enter on its checkout page.

Bank transfer

Bank transfer details are printed on the proforma invoice. Please quote the invoice number in the payment reference.

Bank charges, intermediary fees and currency conversion costs are borne by the buyer; the amount credited to us must equal the invoiced amount.

Other methods

Other payment methods may be agreed in writing before an order is confirmed. Nothing on this site obliges us to accept a method that is not stated on the proforma invoice.

Currency

Invoices are issued in United States dollars unless stated otherwise. Where a buyer pays in another currency, the exchange rate and any conversion cost are agreed in writing before payment.

Late payment and cancellation

If a deposit is not received within the validity period of the invoice, the price may be requoted and the production slot released.

Once material has been cut or a custom mould has been made, an order cannot be cancelled without covering the cost already committed. Where cancellation is accepted, any amount already spent on materials, tooling or approved customisation is deducted from the refund.

Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method wherever possible. Card refunds are processed through Stripe and typically appear within five to ten business days, depending on your bank.

Refunds are made in the currency of the original invoice. We do not reimburse exchange-rate differences or bank charges incurred on the original payment.

Chargebacks

If you believe a payment was taken in error, please contact us at josie@boyarn.com before raising a chargeback with your bank. Most issues are resolved faster that way, and we can usually tell you within one business day what the payment relates to.
